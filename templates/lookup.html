Look up a geometry

Have a manifold you find interesting? Enter its Hodge numbers to get its identifier, its derived invariants, and how it scores in our ranking.

The Euler characteristic is derived from these, not entered — so it always agrees with the Hodge numbers.

What this can and cannot tell you

It can give you a stable identifier for a set of invariants, compute derived quantities (χ, tadpole charge χ/24, total moduli), and show you where comparable geometries land in our ranking.

It cannot tell you whether this is your specific manifold. Hodge numbers do not uniquely determine a Calabi-Yau: many distinct geometries share the same (h1,1, h2,1, χ). The ~474M Kreuzer-Skarke polytopes collapse to only about 30,000 distinct Hodge pairs.

To refer to a geometry unambiguously, cite the Kreuzer-Skarke polytope (its vertex matrix) together with the triangulation used.
