What are these numbers?

Calabi–Yau shapes are candidate “hidden dimensions” in string theory. h¹¹ counts Kähler moduli (sizes/shapes of even cycles); h²¹ counts complex-structure moduli. For a CY threefold, Euler characteristic is forced by χ = 2(h¹¹ − h²¹). The tadpole budget is L = |χ|/24.
